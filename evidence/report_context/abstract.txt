Agricultural forecasting requires consistent annual targets, chronological evaluation and transparent treatment of incomplete reporting. This study analyzes 246,091 crop-production records covering 1997–2015 and constructs annual production-to-area ratios after conservative quality checks. A matched 2005–2014 panel contains 1,112 district–crop histories for Rice, Maize, Wheat and Rapeseed &Mustard. Eight local baseline or classical estimators are compared with a globally tuned XGBoost model, LSTM, GRU and a lightweight patch-based Transformer. Twelve XGBoost candidates use expanding validation targets in 2010–2011; neural epoch selection uses an earlier 2009 stopping year. The validation-selected XGBoost achieves one-step test MAE 0.3369, RMSE 0.5617 and MAPE 24.77% over 2012–2014. LSTM has the lowest retrospective test RMSE, 0.5457, while Naive has the lowest MAPE. Fixed-origin three-step results are reported separately. Prophet was implemented for longer crop-level aggregates but its native optimizer failed locally, so no Prophet accuracy is asserted. Source units, district boundaries and cohort selection limit interpretation. This retrospective extension provides reproducible evidence for model comparison rather than guaranteed operational accuracy or an independent prospective validation.
